I. Notes

slow music made in a damp room.

moss & air is a recording project working out of a cabin north of the fog line. records are built slowly: a hydrophone in a creek, a tape loop left out in the rain, a synth tuned to the room it was recorded in.

the music is quiet but not empty. listen for the insects, the kettle, the cedar floor. everything is on purpose, even the parts that weren't.
