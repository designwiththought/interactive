who is moss & air

moss & air is the recording project of a producer working out of a damp cabin somewhere north of the fog line. the music is built slowly — a hydrophone dropped in a creek, a tape loop left out in the rain, a synth patch tuned to the room it was recorded in.

the records are quiet, but they are not empty. listen for the insects, the kettle, the cedar floor. everything is on purpose, even the parts that weren't.
