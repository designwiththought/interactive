Half a story is a beginning.

Halfheard is a podcast about the stories I only caught the tail end of and went looking for the rest of. Every season is one topic, told in four to six tightly-edited episodes — all released the same day, so you can listen straight through. Written and recorded by Brian, in a small studio south of Market.

A note from Brian

I’m the kind of person who hears half a story at a dinner party and then can’t sleep until I know the rest. Halfheard is what happens when I stop trying to fight it.

Every season I pick one figure or one mystery and I dig — for months. Then I write it up as a series of four to six short presentations and put the whole season out on the same morning, so you can binge it in a long afternoon or stretch it across a week. No co-host. No interviews. Just me, a microphone, and one story I couldn’t let go.

Each episode opens the same way:

“From a small studio in San Francisco — I’m Brian, and you’re listening to Halfheard. This season, six episodes on Timothy Dexter — the most improbable rich man in early American history.”

And then I tell you what I found. Thanks for listening.

— Brian

About the host

Brian

Brian is a writer and former newsroom researcher who spent a decade trying to fact-check stories that nobody had bothered to fact-check yet. He grew up half-listening to his grandfather’s shortwave radio, which is probably where the trouble started.

He lives in San Francisco and records Halfheard from a small studio south of Market — usually at night, usually with the window open, usually after he should be in bed.
