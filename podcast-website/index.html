A note from Mira

I started Kettle Talk in the winter of 2022, mostly because I missed the kind of conversations that don't go anywhere in particular — the ones that wander off, double back, get quiet, and then suddenly land somewhere.

Every Tuesday morning I make tea (usually Assam, sometimes barley if it’s warm) and I sit down with one person for about an hour. I don't send questions ahead of time. I don't edit out the pauses. Some of these have been the best conversations of my year.

Thank you for listening. Pull up a chair.

— Mira

About the host

Mira Solano

Mira is a writer and former public radio producer who spent a decade making longer-than-they-should-be documentaries about gardens, neighborhoods, and people who collect strange things.

She lives in a small apartment in West Oakland with a cat named Pepper and an absurd number of unmatched mugs. Kettle Talk is recorded at her kitchen table, usually on Tuesdays, often with someone’s dog in the background.
