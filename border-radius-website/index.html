Type a radius once, reuse it everywhere

Don’t eyeball corners. Save your radii as number variables so every card, button, and chip rounds the same. Drag below to feel it.

radius/lg 24 px
border-radius: 24px;

A note from the editor

I’ve spent the better part of a decade designing products — and somewhere along the way I became the person on the team everybody pinged with “wait, how do you do that again?” Eventually I started writing the answers down. Border Radius is that pile of answers, cleaned up and sent out once a week.

Some tips are about Figma specifically — auto layout, variables, the shortcuts you keep meaning to learn. Plenty are just about good design: spacing, type, color, and the habits that make the work go faster. And when there’s more to say, I say it out loud on the podcast.

This week: a confession. I built buttons by hand for years before I trusted auto layout. Press Shift A, wrap your frame, and let it do the spacing. You will not go back.

No 90-minute deep dives, no “10 plugins that will change your life,” no autoplay. Just the corners I’ve rounded off so you don’t have to. Thanks for reading.

— Brian
