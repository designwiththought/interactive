What’s the podcast?

Border Radius, out loud.

Same idea as the newsletter, with a microphone. Every other Thursday I take one design idea — a Figma trick, a layout principle, a hard-won process lesson — and talk it all the way through in about twenty minutes. Solo most weeks, with a guest designer when I can twist an arm.

No ads, no meandering. Subscribe wherever you listen:

About the editor

Brian

Brian is a product designer who has shipped design systems at two startups and one company large enough to have a “design ops” team. He has strong, slightly embarrassing opinions about nested components and variable modes.

He writes Border Radius on Sunday nights and records the podcast whenever the room is quiet enough. If something here saved you ten minutes, that’s the whole job done.
